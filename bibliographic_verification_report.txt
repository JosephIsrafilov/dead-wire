   Exact quotes extracted:

   - "In spaced letters count two for the space"
   - "The letter L may be prolonged to count eight and called long dash"
   - "Spaces between words last while counting four"

3. TIMING RATIOS — CRITICAL DISCREPANCY
   Potter 1870 page 7 explicitly states:

   "To make a dot, strike with a hammer as you count one and raise it
   instantly; to make a dash, strike and count four, then raise again.
   The dash taking four times as long as the dot."

   This describes:
   - Dot = count 1
   - Dash = count 4
   - Ratio = 1:4 (NOT 1:3)
