# Third-Party Notices — DEAD WIRE Beta

The package must retain the game's required notices and license texts. This short index does not replace individual license files or the full provenance record.

- **Kenney impact sounds:** CC0. Used for stamp and chair-related impact Foley; voluntary credit is recorded in `docs/third_party_assets.md`.
- **Footstep surface recordings (Phase B):** CC0. rubberduck (*100 CC0 SFX #2*, *25 CC0 mud sfx*), TinyWorlds, Fantozzi (submitted by qubodup), HaelDB (*Footsteps Leather, Cloth, Armor*, dual OGA-BY 3.0 / CC0 — CC0 taken) and Thimras (*Metal footsteps on concrete*), plus mikeask's *Steps in wood floor*. Ten town/interior surface banks are cut from these recordings; sources and per-surface attribution are recorded in `docs/third_party_assets.md`.
- **Luckius paper sound effects:** CC0. Selected paper sample; source and usage are recorded in `docs/third_party_assets.md`.
- **IM FELL English Roman, Cedarville Cursive, Courier Prime:** SIL Open Font License 1.1. The corresponding `OFL.txt` files are bundled beside the fonts and must remain with redistributed font files.
- **Intro music:** the project's own generated drone (`audio/music/intro_drone.wav`); no third-party music ships. The former temporary "Long Note One" (Kevin MacLeod, CC BY 4.0) is unreferenced and excluded.
- **Noto Sans (static Regular), SIL OFL 1.1:** `assets/fonts/noto/NotoSans-Regular.ttf` (variable source kept out of the package under `variable_source/`); UI/control readability. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosans . OFL text in `licenses/fonts/noto/OFL.txt`.
- **Old Standard TT, SIL OFL 1.1:** `assets/fonts/oldstandard/`; period-inspired styling. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oldstandardtt . OFL text in `licenses/fonts/oldstandard/OFL.txt`.
- **Elias Working hand (project-authored bitmap font):** generated from project SVG sources by `tools/build_elias_font.py`; not a third-party font. Artistic status is unaccepted (see the beta report).
- **Temporary beta sounds (project-generated, provisional):** sounder, footstep_wood_beta, register_wind, wick_turn, sheet_slide, paper_turn, route_lever, door_latch and room/ambience beds; all regenerate byte-identically through the project generators listed below. Not final recorded Foley; human listening acceptance is a separate open gate. Footsteps no longer use the synthesised takes: they ship the recorded CC0 surface banks listed above, and the packager refuses `footstep_wood_beta` beside them.
- **Project-authored/generated assets:** listed in the project register.

Provenance status: the sounder down/up samples were closed by byte-identical regeneration through `tools/generate_sounder.py`; action Foley (paper_turn, route_lever, door_latch, ink_stamp, wick_turn, sheet_slide, pen_scratch, key_click, door_hinge) regenerates byte-identically through `tools/generate_action_foley.py`; footsteps ship the recorded CC0 surface banks in the list above, cut by `tools/build_footstep_surface_banks.py`, with the unknown-origin legacy `footstep_wood.wav` excluded from this package (kept in the source repository for rollback only) and the synthesised `footstep_wood_beta.wav` no longer referenced. Luckius paper and Kenney impacts keep their external licenses as listed above; Long Note One does not ship. The remaining generated ambience beds are project-owned temporary sounds, not final recorded Foley; the owner provenance review and human listening pass remain open gates. No blanket legal clearance is claimed.

Preserve all asset-local license files in the runtime package when those assets are included. See `docs/third_party_assets.md` in the source repository for the full register and URLs.
